Session Recap
What has happened in Soleij
▼

Act 1 — The High Archive

The party entered the High Archive and located Albertun — Tu'buks's research partner. Using pressure from the Headmistress's presence, they recovered all three documents Tu'buks had left behind: a stellar alignment chart with red-ink annotations, troll biology research notes, and a torn fragment referencing a name.

Act 2 — Koya's Infiltration

Koya remained behind and transformed into the Headmistress's likeness, compelling Albertun to escort him to the restricted section. Inside, the unmistakeable stench of decay came from behind the west wall. He and Albertun searched but could not find the mechanism. Albertun panicked and fled.

The passage behind the west wall was not opened. The trigger was not found.

Act 3 — The Watch Station

Sael, Palento, and Diego were escorted to the watch station by Captain Torvald Brek, who is investigating Tu'buks's disappearance alongside two dead watchmen. During the interrogation everyone in the room was paralysed. When it passed, the three documents Diego had been carrying were gone.

Act 4 — The Tavern on the Hill

The party spoke with Aldric Wenn — the tavern keeper, a heavyset widower in his fifties who knew Tu'buks personally as a quiet, reliable guest. Aldric was wary but responded to genuine warmth. Tu'buks's old room had been cleaned and re-let to a travelling wool merchant. Behind the headboard the party found a hidden note — a list of four locations in Soleij written in Tu'buks's personal shorthand, each with a date beside it. Aldric then directed the party to meet him out back, where he handed over a box of Tu'buks's remaining belongings: clothes, a writing kit, two books — one of them covered in marginalia throughout, with a name written small on the last page.

Act 5 — The Ambush

The handoff was ambushed by a four-person freelance crew with no identifying marks. A sharpshooter named Thessan opened from a rooftop, targeting Sael specifically and aiming for his throat scar. Sael avoided the first bolt. The second was absorbed by his armour. The party fought and won — three attackers killed, one captured. The captive, Sill, was interrogated and handed to the city watch. Thessan escaped without being seen clearly.

Sill gave up one piece of information: Thessan had a rendezvous point for after the job — north of the city, where two waterfalls meet at a brook.

Before the ambush, passing through the common room, the party noticed a woman sitting alone — very petite, dirty blonde hair, brown eyes — whittling wooden dowels. She did not speak to them or draw attention to herself.

⚿ GM — What actually happened

The watch station theft: Master Vedris Orun used Unnoticeable to enter invisibly and a master-level paralysing ability to freeze everyone. He took all three documents from Diego. Diego alone passed his Resolute test and saw him — paralysed, unable to speak. This knowledge is exclusively Diego's.

The woman in the tavern: Captain Varelia Sorn — QI field operative, alias Ledger. She recognised Sael on sight via Perfect Recall. She activated the ambush crew after leaving the common room. Her crew is now broken. Sorn is reassessing and is one step behind the party for the first time.

The wooden dowels: Sorn's communication method. The party has seen them without knowing their significance.

Critical: Orun has Document 3, which names Kulinan Furia. He does not know the party has the book — which is more dangerous than everything he took at the watch station.

Tu'buks's Book
Player GM Only
The Margins of Convergence
Recovered from Aldric Wenn · Tu'buks's working journal
▼

A mid-sized published academic text — a dry scholarly work on Ambrian land survey methodology that nobody would look twice at. The kind of book a travelling scholar carries as professional camouflage. The cover is worn, the spine cracked from heavy use, and it smells faintly of the archive — old paper, tallow candle, and damp stone. Tu'buks has been using it for years. It came with him from Thistle Hold. The published text is almost irrelevant. The book belongs to the margins now.

Layer One — Old Notes

Academic cross-references, page numbers pointing to other texts, the ordinary working notes of a thorough researcher. Goblins, ogres, troll biology. Astronomical tables. References to Symbarian burial practice. Tu'buks's normal scholarly voice — precise, impersonal, slightly pedantic. Nothing alarming here unless you already know what you're looking for.

Layer Two — The Shift

Somewhere in the middle of the book the handwriting changes. Still Tu'buks, but tighter, faster, the letters compressed like a man writing quickly and not entirely comfortable with what he's writing. This is where the convergence thread begins to appear.

"Dispersal confirmed in at least three independent sources — not one site, many."
"The conjunction window is not approximate. Someone calculated this."
"Furia's name appears again. Third time. Cannot be coincidence."
"The rite does not require a single location. This changes everything."
"If I am right the archive here has been partially cleaned. Someone got here first."

These notes don't form a complete argument — they're the live edge of a mind working through something alarming in real time. A careful reader could reconstruct most of what Tu'buks concluded.

Layer Three — Soleij Specific

The most recent notes, written during his time here. Shorter. More cautious. The handwriting is controlled again — not the precision of a scholar, the precision of someone being careful.

Observations about archive staff — Wendric noted by name as trustworthy. Bettric noted: "watches the restricted section door more than her work warrants."
A rough sketch of the passage west wall with a question mark beside it.
"Administrative office — no name, no register entry. Turned away. Someone is here who should not be." [beside the southern estate address]
The cartographer's name — Helvane Doss — circled, with the word "confirmed" beside it and three lines underneath.
"The gate. Green wheel. Soldiers dressed as merchants. This is how they move. I need to leave Soleij." [final entry — four days before he disappeared]

He did not leave in time.

Final Page — Written separately, in careful unhurried script
"Speak to Osvel. Chapel. He noticed the gap."
⚿ GM — Why this book changes everything

This book is the most dangerous object the party is currently carrying. It contains Furia's name written in Tu'buks's hand connected explicitly to convergence ritual theory, a direct observation of QI logistics movement through the western gate, a notation about the southern estate address pointing directly at the records office, Bettric flagged as suspicious by name, and a path to Osvel at the chapel.

Orun searched Tu'buks's room and missed it — it was in Aldric's box of belongings, not in the room itself. If Orun ever learns the party has this book, everything escalates immediately. It is more valuable and more dangerous than the three documents he took at the watch station.

Bettric flagged: Tu'buks identified her as suspicious before he disappeared, before the room was searched, before the current chaos. If the party connects this to what they already know about the archive, Bettric becomes an immediate and actionable target.

The southern estate address: Tu'buks walked directly to the mission objective without knowing what it was. He noted it as a dead end. It is not.

The gate observation: "Green wheel. Soldiers dressed as merchants." This is QI's logistics signature for moving assets out of Soleij. The party can potentially use this to understand how Tu'buks was moved — and whether the same route is being used again.

Osvel: Junior priest at the Chapel of Radiant Memory — young, eager, slightly bored. He spotted the same liturgical gap in the records that Tu'buks found and never told anyone because he didn't think it mattered. Tu'buks met him, trusted him, and left his name as a breadcrumb. Osvel doesn't know he's been named. He doesn't know Tu'buks is missing. He just knows a quiet scholar came and asked interesting questions about a gap in the records that nobody had ever asked about before.

Four Locations · Four Dates
Found hidden behind the headboard · Written in Tu'buks's shorthand
▼

The dates span approximately three weeks, ending four days before he disappeared.

The Chapel of Radiant Memory
Earliest date — approximately three weeks before disappearance
Tu'buks visited the Chapel's ecclesiastical records. He was cross-referencing astronomical observations against historical Prios liturgical dates. What he found — or didn't find — is unclear. He spoke with at least one member of the chapel staff.
An Address in the Southern Estate District
Approximately two and a half weeks before disappearance
A specific address on the quieter end of the southern estate row. Tu'buks visited in person and was turned away by a clerk who said the office held no historical records. He noted the address and moved on, apparently treating it as a dead end. His book suggests otherwise.
A Private Residence — Scholar's Quarter
Approximately ten days before disappearance
The home of Helvane Doss — a retired cartographer, seventies, nearly blind, formerly one of the most respected mapmakers in Yndarien. Tu'buks had corresponded with her before arriving in Soleij. He visited her personally. He left her house visibly shaken. What she told him is unknown.
The Western Gate Guard Post
Four days before disappearance — the final entry
Tu'buks went to the western gate not to speak to the guards but to watch outbound traffic. He stayed approximately two hours. His book contains a note written that same day: "Green wheel. Soldiers dressed as merchants. This is how they move. I need to leave Soleij."
⚿ GM — What each location actually is

The Chapel: Tu'buks found a gap in the liturgical records — a period where the astronomical observations simply stop, then resume as if nothing happened. Osvel noticed it too. The gap corresponds to the Symbarian period and has been quietly maintained for a very long time. What it hides is a GM decision, but it connects to the convergence timeline.

The Southern Estate Address: The QI records office. Tu'buks walked directly to the mission objective without knowing what it was. The clerk who turned him away was QI staff. This address in Tu'buks's shorthand is the clearest pointer the party currently has to their actual objective.

Helvane Doss: She told Tu'buks about a buried structure approximately a mile and a half southwest of Soleij beneath a low ridge locals call the Grey Back. This is what shook him. She knows it from her years of cartographic survey work — she mapped the ridge and found anomalies she could not explain and eventually stopped asking about. What the structure actually is remains a GM decision.

The Western Gate: Tu'buks observed QI logistics — the "green wheel" is a cart marking used by QI supply lines running merchant cover. He recognised soldiers posing as merchants. This is how they move people and materials in and out of Soleij without drawing attention. The party could potentially use this intelligence to identify QI movements or trace where Tu'buks was taken.

The High Archive
Restricted Section · The Passage · What Was Found
▼

The institutional heart of Soleij. Ecclesiastical records and marked volumes on the east wall. A bronze disc mechanism on the west wall — its purpose not yet understood by the party.

What the party recovered

Document 1 — Stellar Alignment Chart

A pre-existing archive astronomical survey of the Andonia-Tomol conjunction cycle, covered in red-ink annotations. Two thresholds marked: approximately 18 months, and approximately 24 months. Marginal note: "Not a prophecy. A timetable. Someone built this on purpose."

Document 2 — Research Notes (Troll Biology)

Personal working notes on goblin-ogre-troll biological transition. Midway through, in different ink: "if dispersal is the mechanism, a rite need not happen in one place — it can happen in many simultaneously." Last entry: "I should not write this down. [written down anyway]"

Document 3 — The Torn Fragment

References a name and "distributed archive nodes, no single point of failure." A hand-drawn seven-point schema — inverted containment, the centre is an entrance. Note: "Not Symbarian. Too recent. Someone is rebuilding the wheel."

All three documents were taken at the watch station. The party retains only what they remember reading.

⚿ GM — View Original Documents

Now held by Orun. Shown below for GM reference only.

⚿ GM — The Passage (Unsolved · Requires Two People)

The passage behind the west wall remains sealed. Requires two people. Wendric's body is inside at approximately 25 paces. Passage exits in Baron Jesavar's estate wine cellar.

Stage 1 — The Lintel: "The eye of Prios opens at dawn, at zenith, and at the last light before dark." Dawn = east wall. Zenith = ceiling. Last light = west wall.

Stage 2 — East Wall: The marble quarry ledger has no dust. Pulling it reveals a scratched sun symbol. Three sun-marked volumes extended simultaneously. First click.

Stage 3 — West Wall: Press the lower corner of the correct panel to reveal the bronze disc. Rotate to ZENITH. Second click. Ceiling gold-leaf ray (midday only) points west to confirm.

Stage 4 — The Spoken Phrase: "Sarathos edivar Prios, mekana vel thodrim" — from the Solstice Eye prayer in chapel records. Spoken aloud with sequence active, the passage opens.

Inside: 40 paces total. Iron pin holds door open. Midpoint lock at 15 paces. Wendric's body at 25 paces, one hand extended toward the midpoint lock.

Also untouched in the archive: The Jeseebegai Document (hidden in genealogical records). The Seventh Convergence Node (misfiled under agricultural drainage records, Year 11).
