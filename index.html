Session Recap
What has happened in Soleij
▼

Act 1 — The High Archive

The party entered the High Archive and located Albertun — Tu'buks's research partner. Using pressure from the Headmistress's presence, they recovered all three documents Tu'buks had left behind: a stellar alignment chart with red-ink annotations, troll biology research notes containing an unexpected discovery, and a torn fragment referencing a name.

Act 2 — Koya's Infiltration

Koya remained behind to surveil Albertun while the others found lodging. Using his Shapeshifter ability he transformed into the Headmistress's likeness and compelled Albertun to escort him to the restricted section. Inside, Koya detected the unmistakeable stench of something decaying behind the west wall. He and Albertun searched the shelving but could not find any mechanism. Then Albertun panicked and fled, and Koya was forced to pursue him rather than continue searching.

The passage behind the west wall was not opened. The trigger was not found.

Act 3 — The Watch Station

Sael, Palento, and Diego were confronted by the city watch at the archive entrance and escorted to the watch station for questioning. Captain Torvald Brek is investigating the disappearance of Tu'buks on the same night two of his watchmen died. He is professionally suspicious and made no secret of it. During the interrogation, everyone in the room was paralysed. When it passed, the three documents Diego had been carrying were gone.

Act 4 — The Tavern on the Hill

The party found lodging at the Tavern on the Hill — the same establishment where Tu'buks stayed during his time in Soleij. This is where the session ended.

⚿ GM — What actually happened

Master Vedris Orun used Unnoticeable to enter the watch station invisibly, then used a master-level paralysing ability to freeze everyone present. He relieved Diego of all three documents and departed without trace. Diego alone passed his Resolute test and was aware — though paralysed — of the theft as it happened. He saw the outline of something that should not have been there. This knowledge is exclusively Diego's for now.

Critical: Orun now has Document 3, which names Kulinan Furia. He is in damage-control mode and will move against the party.

The High Archive
Restricted Section · The Passage · What Was Found
▼

The institutional heart of Soleij. Ecclesiastical records and marked volumes on the east wall. A bronze disc mechanism on the west wall — its purpose not yet understood by the party.

What the party recovered

Document 1 — Stellar Alignment Chart

A pre-existing archive astronomical survey of the Andonia-Tomol conjunction cycle, covered in red-ink annotations. Two thresholds marked: approximately 18 months, and approximately 24 months. Marginal note: "Not a prophecy. A timetable. Someone built this on purpose."

Document 2 — Research Notes (Troll Biology)

Personal working notes on goblin-ogre-troll biological transition. Midway through, in different ink: "if dispersal is the mechanism, a rite need not happen in one place — it can happen in many simultaneously." Last entry: "I should not write this down. [written down anyway]"

Document 3 — The Torn Fragment

References a name and "distributed archive nodes, no single point of failure." A hand-drawn seven-point schema — inverted containment, the centre is an entrance. Note: "Not Symbarian. Too recent. Someone is rebuilding the wheel."

All three documents were taken at the watch station. The party retains only what they remember reading.

⚿ GM — The Passage (Unsolved · Requires Two People)

The passage behind the west wall remains sealed. Requires two people to operate. Wendric's body is inside at approximately 25 paces. The passage exits in Baron Jesavar's estate wine cellar.

Stage 1 — The Lintel: "The eye of Prios opens at dawn, at zenith, and at the last light before dark." Dawn = east wall. Zenith = ceiling. Last light = west wall.

Stage 2 — East Wall: The marble quarry ledger has no dust. Pulling it reveals a scratched sun symbol. Three sun-marked volumes extended simultaneously. First click.

Stage 3 — West Wall: Press the lower corner of the correct panel to reveal the bronze disc. Rotate to ZENITH. Second click. Ceiling gold-leaf ray (midday only) points west to confirm.

Stage 4 — The Spoken Phrase: "Sarathos edivar Prios, mekana vel thodrim" — from the Solstice Eye prayer in chapel records. Spoken aloud with sequence active, the passage opens.

Inside: 40 paces total. Iron pin holds door open. Midpoint lock at 15 paces. Wendric's body at 25 paces, one hand extended toward the midpoint lock he could not reach.

Also untouched in the archive: The Jeseebegai Document (hidden in genealogical records). The Seventh Convergence Node (misfiled under agricultural drainage records, Year 11).

GM Key Notes — The Night of the Incident
Full chronology · Wendric's account · What each faction did
▼
⚿ GM — Full Chronology

Early evening — QI moves: Four masked QI agents enter the archive late in the evening, moving with purpose. They kill Sera Dunt and Bram Ossil at the main entry desk quickly and quietly. Wendric is walking the outside grounds and does not see this. He does see the four agents enter and, already suspicious — he has recently found a cipher letter stashed in a restricted collection book by an unknown figure one evening, pocketed it impulsively, and has been trying to decode it — he decides to tail them inside.

The extraction: The agents navigate directly to the restricted section as if they know the building. Bettric is already hiding near the stairhead — she heard something was wrong. Wendric and Bettric join up silently and follow the agents down. They watch as the four agents work through the four-stage passage unlock sequence — requiring two people — then usher Tu'buks out. He is bound, hooded, at daggerpoint. The passage closes behind them.

The decision: Wendric and Bettric, in hushed panic, decide to follow. They repeat the unlock sequence and open the passage themselves. They enter.

Orun acts: Bettric has been quietly updating Orun through the enchanted earring she wears — she does not know the earring is the mechanism. He takes remote puppet control of her body. Under his command, Bettric stabs Wendric and leaves him in the passage. Orun's logic: no witnesses, no loose threads. He does not yet know the agents are QI — only that something significant is happening involving Tu'buks and he wants it contained.

The exit: Still under Orun's partial control, Bettric follows the passage to its end — Baron Jesavar's estate wine cellar. The QI agents are already gone with Tu'buks. Bettric is found shortly after wandering the estate grounds, disoriented, with no coherent explanation. Some of Jesavar's staff noticed. This is a dormant clue.

Wendric bleeds out alone in the passage at approximately 25 paces. His body, the original partially-decoded Unblinded cipher letter he pocketed from the restricted collection, and whatever else he carried are still on his person. He never found out who placed the letter or who was meant to retrieve it.

Same night in the city: The QI agents encounter Watchmen Aldus Pren and Coris Fael somewhere on their route out of Soleij — witnesses who had to be silenced. Both are killed.

Separately — at the Tavern on the Hill: Sael and Kira (the changeling posing as Kira Anderisi) go to the archive looking for Tu'buks when he fails to appear. He is not there. They rush back to his room at the Tavern on the Hill. He is gone. While they search his room in panic, the changeling finds copies of materials — evidence of a large-scale ritual effort not seen in generations, and something suggesting a senior Ordo Magica voice knows the Dark Lord still lives. She takes the materials. Then she turns on Sael — cuts his throat along the old scar and leaves him for dead. She disappears into the city.

The next day — Bettric's problem: Back under her own control but operating under Orun's orders, Bettric returns to the restricted section intending to deal with Wendric's body. She cannot open the passage alone — two people required. She panics, then resolves to play dumb. The restricted section is already off-limits. She tells Orun she cannot access the body. He accepts this for now. The smell begins. She says nothing, takes the same ignorant stance as Albertun, and hopes the problem stays sealed.

The cultist who placed the dead-drop letter in the restricted collection has not been able to retrieve it. Whoever they are, they know the letter is missing. That thread is still live.
